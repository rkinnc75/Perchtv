Privacy

PerchTV (on Android TV and Samsung televisions) and Perch (on Android phones). Updated 4 October 2026.

PerchTV plays, on your television, a video that a phone you paired with it sends. Perch is a phone app that sends the videos stored on your phone. Neither app has accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking, and neither sells or shares anything about you.

What leaves your devices

On your devices

Perch asks to see the videos on your phone so it can list them. It reads them only to show them to you and to send the one you choose. It never changes or deletes them. You can instead pick a single file with the system's file picker, which needs no permission. Each app keeps its pairing on the device until you unpair.

Unpairing and deleting

Unpair from Perch, or hold OK on the television's remote. Unpairing ends the pairing on the television, on the phone and in the pairing service. Uninstalling either app removes everything it kept on that device.

Children

The apps are not directed at children and collect nothing that identifies a person.
