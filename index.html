PerchTV and Perch

PerchTV plays videos on your television that you send from your phone. Perch is the phone app that sends the videos stored on your phone.

Getting started

  1. Install PerchTV on your television and open it. It shows nine digits.
  2. Install Perch on your phone, open it, and enter those digits and a name for the television.
  3. Pick a video on your phone. It plays on the television. Stop it from the phone, or with Back on the remote.

Your phone and television must be on the same Wi-Fi network. To unpair, tap Unpair in Perch, or hold OK on the television's remote.
